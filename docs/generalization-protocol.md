# Generalization v1: fixed geometric accuracy before memory comparison

These are research acceptance thresholds, not application tolerances or a continuous worst-case certificate. All earlier shapes, parameters, orientations and seeds are development evidence. The final candidate and complete measurement scope are frozen before any final representation is fitted.

The fixed candidate starts with a depth-five q8 tree and one global coverage point in every depth-five cell. It ranks at most 4,096 depth-five parents by decoded q8 residual SSE on the original 10,000 fitting points plus global coverage, refines those parents to depth six, and adds four-point-per-axis quadrature inside them. It then ranks at most 2,048 existing depth-six terminal parents on that full pool, refines them to depth seven, and adds four-point-per-axis quadrature inside those parents. Exact SSE allocation uses all declared labels with equal unit weight and no sign penalty. Global coverage occurs once in the allocation pool; duplicate coordinates retain repeated sample weight. Final feature labels never enter selection or allocation.

The first bounded development screen tested 12 predeclared depth-six policies on five familiar torus/gap instances, fitting seeds 40/41, all 11 original caps, and shared 10,000 uniform plus 10,000 near queries. No policy passed all five. All 1,320 fields and their original classifications were independently verified. Before the final freeze, the sampled-maximum rule was clarified to include feature-probe distances. Original raws and classifications remain unchanged, with a separate amended flag. Independent regular-grid interpolation and decoding showed a depth-six resolution floor. A second bounded screen tested the p08 control and K7 = 128, 512 and 2,048, totaling 440 fields. It ranked policies by the number of geometries passing both seeds at the same cap, then actual crossing bytes plus a 512 KiB selection-only penalty per uncrossed geometry, then oracle requests, construction time and fixed order. The penalty is not a memory crossing. The selected K7 = 2,048 policy passed full 100,000 uniform plus 100,000 near confirmation on all five development instances and both fitting seeds, including independent ranks, quadrature coordinates/labels, objectives, bytes and geometric gates. No additional policy expansion or tuning follows this confirmation.

The immutable `runs/generalization-v1/FROZEN.json` records all 16 geometry instances, proper rotations and translations, original mesh similarity transforms, source revisions/hashes/licenses, seeds, witnesses, rays, reference lattice hashes, complete source hashes and executable hash. The domain is [-1,1]^3 with L = 2. Meshes are uniformly scaled so that a sphere centered at their bounding-box midpoint has radius 0.75, then receive the frozen rotation/translation. Upstream OBJ units are unspecified; all conversion factors are recorded. Triangles are preserved without repair, remeshing, decimation or triangulation changes.

The suite contains six familiar-family parameter/orientation stresses (thin boxes, tori and separated spheres), six instances from four novel analytic families (capsule, capped cylinder, rounded box and spherical shell), and four externally sourced real meshes. A rotated torus is not an unseen analytic family. Accepted meshes are goathead, penguin, Spot low resolution and Brucewick from the pinned odedstein/meshes revision. Wingnut was rejected by unchanged EPECK self-intersection validation before any final field fitting, and the input/rejection log is preserved. Spot is public domain. Individual upstream READMEs provide attribution/license evidence; Brucewick is CC BY-NC 4.0 and this is research use.

Every representation must satisfy all gates together:

- Inside and outside sign recall each at least 95%, separately in every declared feature group.
- Mean absolute relative interval-width error at most 10% and p95 at most 15%, separately in every ray group, with no missing/extra roots, missing/zero widths or incorrect ordered signed intervals. Both solid widths and intervening gaps are included.
- Closed edge-and-vertex manifold extraction and the reference component Euler characteristics at both qualified extraction levels.
- Sampled maximum absolute distance error at most 0.020 in **every** saved uniform, near, probe, common-audit and boundary-audit set.
- Uniform CVaR99 at most 0.015 and near CVaR99 at most 0.010.

No gates are relaxed after evaluation. Ray sign transitions at discontinuities are counted; continuity is an additional diagnostic, not a gate. Correct reference interval and endpoint witnesses are required. A failed earlier gate can skip extraction only with `not_evaluated_due_to_prior_failure`, which never counts as a topology pass or an observed topology failure. `oracle_resolution_unresolved` is retained separately and cannot certify a representation pass.

Reference features are frozen before fitting. Independent NumPy exact-SDF formulas verify analytic roots. Actual triangle intersections determine mesh ray roots, and the chosen reference oracle checks each alternating signed interval and witness; very short reference intervals cannot disappear in a fixed scan. Every group has inside and outside witnesses away from zero. Imported meshes use 48 rays with the smallest unambiguous adjacent reference intervals and two groups of 24 rays distributed across remaining interval sizes. These are sequence partitions, not certified semantic anatomical regions. Per-group aggregation prevents large intervals from averaging away selected thin intervals. Analytic rays cover thin thickness, tube diameters, component gaps, capsule ends, cylinder radial/axial dimensions, rounded-box directions and shell/cavity dimensions.

Input topology is independently checked for oriented edge pairs and a single cyclic vertex link, then validated by the unchanged CGAL EPECK loader for closure, triangles, degeneracies and self-intersections. The first consecutive reference-valid extraction pair from {64,96,128,192} is frozen, with cached oracle lattice values and saved meshes. Fifteen inputs qualified at 64/96. Penguin remained unresolved through 192 and is retained explicitly. No input is replaced because a representation fails.

Real-mesh evaluation uses an explicitly audited EPICK AABB distance/sign path after EPECK input validation. EPICK has exact geometric predicates, while constructions are generally inexact; it is not an exact-construction global distance oracle. Each accepted mesh received at least 8,192 stratified EPECK/EPICK paired queries plus every input vertex, face center, edge center, two-sided face sample and frozen witness. Maximum observed distance differences were at most 3.89e-16 domain units, below the predeclared 1e-10 threshold, with no unexplained sign difference outside the 1e-9 tiny band and matching surface samplers. Outputs, group counts and independent brute-force checks are retained. This sampled infrastructure comparison is not a global exactness guarantee. EPECK comparison time includes sampling and labeling; EPICK time measures distances on saved points. Their ratio is not a matched query-speed benchmark. The earlier incomplete EPECK goathead qualification is preserved. See the official [EPICK kernel](https://doc.cgal.org/6.2/Kernel_23/classCGAL_1_1Exact__predicates__inexact__constructions__kernel.html) and [AABB tree](https://doc.cgal.org/latest/AABB_tree/) documentation.

All eight final methods receive the same original fitting pool and noise rule: uniform f32 grid; depth-five q8 exact MSE; depth-five q8 sign penalty 0.0025; depth-five q8 finite-threshold CVaR99 (nine uniform thresholds plus MSE-field p99); selective depth-six MSE K = 32; original candidate K = 128/sign penalty 0.001/parent q4; p08 coverage-only depth-six SSE control (K6 = 4,096, global q1, covered ranking, parent q4, K7 = 0); and the selected candidate. The p08 control is included in the optimistic strongest-q8 envelope. Additional candidate construction labels and their costs are separate from the original fitting pool; total oracle costs are not equal across methods.

Before unseen fitting, the cap grid was uniformly extended to 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128, 192, 256, 512 and 1,024 KiB without altering development ranges or method settings. A familiar torus 0.035/seed-40 pilot measured the 1 MiB frontiers for p08, selected candidate and CVaR, recording time, states, transitions and OS process peak memory. No adaptive cap expansion follows final results. Actual retained object-plus-buffer bytes include descriptors and metadata, with native ABI sizes recorded. Oracle objects, training/reference samples, construction scratch, label caches and allocator bookkeeping are excluded and reported separately. The deterministic grid is built once per instance/cap and reused across five fitting seeds 240–244. Five fitting seeds are replications, not five independent shapes.

Each geometry caches 100,000 uniform and 100,000 near queries in an independent evaluation namespace. The common audit combines a 25^3 lattice and two-sided points around every possible depth-six interface plane. A representation-specific audit deterministically subsamples at most 4,096 two-sided retained leaf-face centers (or grid-interface points), including selected depth-seven interfaces. These labels are obtained after construction and cannot affect fitting. Nine serial warm query batches exclude oracle work, I/O and extraction; raw repeats and checksums are retained.

The shared oracle cache keys all three IEEE binary64 coordinate bit patterns and binds persisted files to a SHA-256 identity containing geometry spec, normalized mesh hash and selected kernel. Loading rejects identity mismatch, truncated records, nonfinite/out-of-domain values and conflicting duplicate labels. Incomplete caches are quarantined and rebuilt. Jobs distinguish logical requests, cache hits, physical evaluations, load time and construction/boundary costs. Shared construction times are cached timings, not cold isolated measurements. Logical requests describe work without reuse. Sample weights remain unchanged and fields retain no oracle/cache pointer.

Geometry/seed/method jobs are immutable and resumable only with matching frozen source, executable, protocol and input/reference hashes. Completed jobs have hash manifests and atomic COMPLETE markers; incomplete folders are quarantined. The independent verifier checks the exact Cartesian product, every retained layout/byte count, predictions, metrics, groups, intervals, ranks, objectives and saved topology. Imported distances receive a brute-force point-triangle and ray-parity audit on a representative subset. A separately rebuilt source snapshot reproduces selected crossings and adjacent failures, covering every accepted real mesh and method. Fields and predictions must agree exactly; timings are remeasured.

Report the smallest **tested actual retained bytes** passing all gates, with no interpolation, monotonicity assumption or exact-minimum claim. Noncrossings remain censored through 1,024 KiB. Show each q8 comparator and an optimistic post-evaluation per-case envelope, which is not a deployable policy. Report per-seed crossing medians/ranges, common caps where every seed passes, and higher-cap failures. **No finite or percentage saving is computed when a comparator has no observed crossing.** Failing tested caps does not certify a continuous minimum beyond the ceiling. Both failing methods demonstrate no saving. Separate familiar stresses, novel families and real meshes, with conditional positive comparisons and their denominators alongside failures. Negative evidence cannot support an achieved lower-memory CV claim.
